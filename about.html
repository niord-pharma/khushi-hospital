About Us

About Khushi Hospital & Laparoscopy Centre

A general & laparoscopic surgery and maternity hospital serving Dalsinghsarai and the surrounding villages of Samastipur district, Bihar.

Hospital Profile

Surgical & Maternity Care in Dalsinghsarai

Khushi Hospital & Laparoscopy Centre is located on Hospital Road, next to the Sub-Divisional Hospital in Dalsinghsarai, Samastipur. The hospital provides general surgery, laparoscopic (keyhole) surgery, maternity services including normal and caesarean delivery, general medicine OPD and round-the-clock emergency care.

The hospital has an air-conditioned operation theatre, private inpatient rooms, and 24-hour electricity and water backup. It is empanelled under the Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) scheme, so eligible cardholders can receive cashless treatment for covered procedures.
