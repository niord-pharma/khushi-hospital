About the Hospital

Khushi Hospital & Laparoscopy Centre

Located on Hospital Road, next to the Sub-Divisional Hospital in Dalsinghsarai, Samastipur, Khushi Hospital & Laparoscopy Centre is a registered general & laparoscopic surgery and maternity hospital (Reg. No. 100/22 (R)).

The hospital runs an air-conditioned operation theatre, a labour / delivery room, and private inpatient rooms, with 24-hour electricity and water backup and round-the-clock emergency cover. It is empanelled under Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY).

  • Dalsinghsarai, Samastipur
  • Reg. No. 100/22 (R)
  • 24/7 Emergency
