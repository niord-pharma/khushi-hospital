What We Offer

Services & Treatments

An overview of the surgical, maternity and medical services available at Khushi Hospital & Laparoscopy Centre. Please call +91 99342 55831 to confirm availability, scheduling and charges for a specific procedure.

General Surgery

Consultation and surgery for common general-surgical conditions such as hernia, appendicitis, hydrocele, and piles / fissure. [Confirm the exact list of procedures offered.]

Laparoscopic (Keyhole) Surgery

Minimally invasive surgery through small incisions, which can mean less pain and a quicker return home for suitable patients. [Confirm which laparoscopic procedures are performed.]

Maternity & Delivery

Antenatal check-ups, normal (vaginal) delivery, and caesarean section, with an anaesthetist available for operative deliveries.

General Medicine (OPD)

Outpatient consultation, diagnosis and treatment for common acute and long-term illnesses such as fever, infections, blood pressure and diabetes.

Anaesthesia

Pre-anaesthetic assessment and operation-theatre anaesthesia provided by a qualified anaesthetist (DA).

24/7 Emergency

Round-the-clock cover for acute surgical and medical emergencies, including initial assessment, stabilisation and emergency operations where required. For a life-threatening emergency, come to the hospital directly or call an ambulance on 108.

Inpatient & Post-Operative Care

Private rooms with nursing care, dressings, IV medication and post-operative monitoring. 24-hour electricity and water backup.

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

The hospital is empanelled under PM-JAY. Eligible cardholders can receive cashless treatment for covered procedures — please bring your Ayushman card and a photo ID.

Diagnostics

Basic pre-operative and routine investigations. [Confirm whether these are in-house or arranged through a partner laboratory.]
