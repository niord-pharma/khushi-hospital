Why Choose Us

Hospital-Level Care in Dalsinghsarai

Surgery, maternity and emergency care under one roof, with round-the-clock cover.

Laparoscopic (Keyhole) Surgery

Minimally invasive surgery through small incisions, which can mean less pain and a faster recovery for suitable cases.

Air-Conditioned Operation Theatre

A temperature-controlled OT for planned and emergency procedures, with anaesthesia support.

24/7 Emergency Services

Round-the-clock cover for acute surgical and medical emergencies.

Maternity Unit

Normal delivery and caesarean section, with an anaesthetist available for operative deliveries.

Private Rooms

Private inpatient rooms with 24-hour electricity and water backup.
